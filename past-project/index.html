La Etiqueta · Expectations & Etiquette

How we'll show up, together.

The practical notes. How to dress, what's covered, and how we'll spend the afternoons.

Professional Conduct

  • Hold a professional demeanor in shared spaces and during all sessions.
  • Be respectful of colleagues' time and space.
  • Engage thoughtfully in discussions and workshops.

Dress Code

  • Daytime. The casual side of business casual, resort appropriate.
  • Meals & evening. Smart casual or business casual. No athletic wear.
  • Beach & pool. Appropriate resort attire.

Per Diem

  • $350 per person to put towards an activity of your choice.
  • Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are covered, charged to your room.
  • Anything beyond reasonable should go on your credit card.

Free Time

  • Afternoons from 3 PM onward are yours. Relax at the beach, use the spa, explore, or rest.
  • Optional group activities may be planned. Participation is welcome but not required.
